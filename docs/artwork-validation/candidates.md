# Real-artwork validation: candidate manifest (awaiting approval)

Status: **proposed, not downloaded.** Nothing below has been fetched, imported or
processed. Metadata was read on 2026-11-04 from each file's Wikimedia Commons page
(API `imageinfo`); the NARA catalog records named there were not opened separately.

Purpose: testing PuzzlePress's preparation and coloring preflight on real line art,
and one physical proof. **Not for publication or sale**: commercial use would need
its own rights check, and nothing here is evidence that a file may be published.

## Rights basis used

- **US publication before 1931.** US copyright for works published before
  1 January 1931 has expired (as of 2026). Every patent drawing below was issued
  1861-1911, so it rests on that, not on the USPTO's general statement that patent
  drawings are "typically not subject to copyright restrictions" (USPTO guidance
  cites exceptions in 37 CFR 1.71(d)-(e) and 1.84(s); a drawing could carry a notice).
  Commons tags the NARA scans "PD US Government" (17 USC 105). That tag is doubtful
  for drawings made by applicants or their draftsmen, but the expired term covers them.
- **Artists dead more than 70 years** (Van Gogh 1890, Doré 1883, Tenniel 1914,
  F. S. Meyer 1926): also public domain in life+70 countries.
- **Photographs and scans of two-dimensional public-domain works** are treated as
  public domain in the US (*Bridgeman v. Corel*, 1999) and in the EU (DSM Directive
  art. 14). Some other countries differ; this matters only outside the US.

## What approval would allow

On your approval of specific numbers, PuzzlePress will download only those files,
each into its own new folder outside the repository, record the downloaded bytes'
SHA-256 beside this manifest, and run `tests/artwork_review.py` over them. Originals
are never modified. The review sheet keeps every human-judgement field "not
recorded" until you fill it in.
